Quick answers.

Still stuck? Give us a bell on (07) 3062 7779.

Should I restore my roof or replace it?

If the structure is sound and the issues are surface level, such as faded coating, moss or tired pointing, a restoration is usually the better value. If sheets are rusted through, tiles are brittle or the roof is asbestos, replacement makes more sense. We'll inspect it and give you a straight answer.

How much does a new Colorbond roof cost in Brisbane?

It depends on roof size, pitch, access, what's coming off and the extras you choose. We measure up and give you a clear, itemised quote for free.

Can you help with storm damage after hours?

Yes. We're open 24/7. Call (07) 3062 7779 and we'll get the roof made safe, then sort the permanent fix.

Do you remove asbestos roofing?

Yes. We remove and dispose of old asbestos cement roofing and replace it with new metal sheeting.

Do you do commercial and industrial roofs?

Yes. From shopfront awnings to full warehouse re-roofs, including insulation.
